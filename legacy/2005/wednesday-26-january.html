Wednesdag 26 January

14:00h Presentation

Because we had to go to the exhibition at 17:00, we did the presentation at 2o' clock. Discussions were heavy, causing really strange looks from the audience...

17:00h : Exhibition time.

There was an exhibition on illustration called "ALKUPALAT" (appetizers) done by third-year students of the institute. So we went there!

HÄME-GALLERIA 21.7-13.2.2005
Hämeenkatu 26, 15140 LAHTI, p. 828 2870
he-pe 12-18, la-su ma-ti suljetttu

20:00h : Let's drink!

We went to Teerenpeli. They had this really, terribly bad performance theatre with a transvestite and some popular finnish songs. We learned the finnish how to say "Go Home" ("Gonoroaus") and they shouted that to the performers, who didn't understand of course.

Other words they learned: * Makkisesafke (Can I have a cigaret) * Ik zen ... (I am/my name is) * Amai amai, zoe woaie (It's really windy!)
