Machination

  • Machination contains the following letters: M, A, C, H, I, N, A, T, I, O, N and Y.
  • Begin by typing a word with these letters. They appear in their disguised, closed form.
  • Now annoy them by clicking on them a lot. Once they are annoyed enough, they will open up.
  • You can begin a new word by typing the backspace key.
  • Some words trigger special actions. Type "contaminate" and hit the space bar.
