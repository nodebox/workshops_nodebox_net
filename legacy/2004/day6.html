Office Man™

Office Man, apparrently, is a spin-off action figure for the Spider Man movie. However, you can immediately see how desperate the marketing department was when they had to find good action figures. The best they could come up with, after they did Spiderman and The Green Goblin, was Office Man.

Sitting at his desk, office man is not afraid of tight deadlines, empty staplers or ringing telephones. And when anyone dares to enter his private domain, he'll use his pounding fist to show them the door.

Yes, that's right. Office Man has "desk pounding action". It's pretty amazing, and I'm sure a lot of adventures will be had when the fist of Office Man starts pounding the desk. You know he's though. You know he's ready. And you know he's up for some coffee, when the objects on his desk start to rattle.©

Office Man was a €5 special at the Sokos supermarket. It was already priced off 70%, for evident reasons. Because who would buy an action figure of a man with a desk?! I mean, think of it, with heroes like spiderman, you could imagine some stories around them. But with a guy and his desk?

So Tom bought it! And he loves his Office Man dearly...
