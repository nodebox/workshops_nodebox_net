Today is production day! (and probably night) By now, the students have pretty solid ideas on what to do, and are creating lots and lots and lots of PDF files. Once again, the assignment isn't easy: by tommorrow noon, they should have a book that counts 724 pages about automation.

Luckily they have DrawBot. In the last few days, it has proven its worth as a production tool. It now keeps getting better and better: the export command now does auto-numbering, and a multi-page export is on its way. (If only Frederik could get those damn PyObjC bugs squashed!)
