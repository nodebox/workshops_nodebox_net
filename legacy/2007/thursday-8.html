Today is Thursday, 12 february 2007

09:00 — Presentation

It started with NightWish: have a look Wishmaster

Followed by a presentation of the Dublin AD Lib cd-rom, a 4-day workshop in 2002. The emphasis was on embedding emotional information in typefaces. Also “The Battle” was part of this project, where fonts could battle with basic emotions. AD LIB.

Have a look at the educational concept of these workshops: Ideas from the Heart.

Then NodeBox was shown: http://nodebox.net

Special attention to all the NodeBox type tricks, see the Gallery too.

The Assignment

Design, choose, create a meme. Analyse the way this meme will influence typefaces in all design aspects. Learn FontLab and create a font with your memes characteristics or design a system on how to influence the characteristics of another typeface.
