SORRY i couldn’t make it to the final presentation, but i had to leave Lahti and travel to the middle territories of Finland. Anyway, the font was finished on thursday 15th, but it lacks ligatures and numbers. The workshop was excellent the times i actually was there.

My meme is buttons(clothes accessories). They give information of who’s carrying them: his/her personality and ideas. Buttons unite, they advertise, they make a perfect ground for opinions.

Culturally, use of buttons differ from what kind of people you represent. There’s a difference between midwest american hillbilly’s baseball cap buttons and a punk rocker’s uniform jacket buttons.

Buttons have pins and they usually have a round form. There’s different sizes and different messages. Multiple buttons form an army of buttons and some structure or pattern. This is something that i should think while making the font.

“BUTUNZ”

The design is out of forms and shapes of buttons and safety pins.
